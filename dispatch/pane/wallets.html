These numbers are read from the chain by this page's server, not reported by the agent. The agent pays out of RLUSD it has escrowed in a payment channel, so its own wallet balance stays flat while the channel balance falls. The merchant balance rises only once the payment settles, which can take several seconds after the voucher is redeemed. The merchant wallet is shared by every seller on this team, so a rise that no run explains is worth checking before you present.
